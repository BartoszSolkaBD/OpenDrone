What do Skate Park and Bando look like?

Three rough blockouts per Map for #17. Each one is a Python script run by headless Blender 5.2, exported to a .glb with collider tags (the pipeline from #8), then rendered. React to layout, size, features, Launch Spots and style: the grey and orange colours are only blockout colours. Mix freely: "B's bowls with A's stairs" is a fine answer.

How to read the images

  • Grid: thin lines every 1 m, bold lines every 5 m, on every surface. White box figures are 1.8 m people, for scale only (not in the .glb).
  • Markers: round numbers are features (listed under the images). Square L1–L3 are Launch Spot candidates; ★ is my pick. Every Map gets exactly one Launch Spot, so pick one per Map.
  • FPV shots: a plain 110° horizontal lens. Landed camera height 3.5 cm on the whoop, 7 cm on the 5". Camera Tilt 20° on the whoop, 30° on the 5". All placeholders until the FPV-camera ticket (#14) sets them.
  • Crossing times use rough cruise speeds (estimates): whoop 5 m/s, 5" 20 m/s, corner to corner of the Map's core.
  • Lightmap texel is an estimate: the core's surface area packed into one lightmap at 65% efficiency. Smaller is sharper baked shadow.
