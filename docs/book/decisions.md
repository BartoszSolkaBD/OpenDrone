# Decisions

Each decision that's hard to undo has an architecture decision record, or ADR: what was decided, the options that were weighed and why they lost, and what follows from it. They're numbered in the order they were made, and listed under this page.

Where a decision has an ADR, the ADR is the authority: the spec and the tickets point to it rather than repeat it. An ADR is never quietly changed. Editing one is a Red Flag that waits for the maintainer ([Development](../context/development.md)), so a change of mind gets a new ADR that says which one it replaces.
